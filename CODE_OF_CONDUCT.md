# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit and structure of the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1
(<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>), which is licensed under CC BY 4.0. This is a
shortened adaptation.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to open source or to the code.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person, and say what would make it better.
- Assume good intent and ask a question before assuming a mistake was careless.
- Accept responsibility when something you did caused a problem, and fix it.
- Credit other people's ideas and contributions.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, derogatory comments, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as an email or address, without permission.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption of discussions, or repeated off-topic demands.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when someone is representing the
project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit or reject comments,
commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow them, and may restrict participation for behaviour they
judge inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

To report a problem, use GitHub's private reporting on this repository (the Security tab, "Report a vulnerability",
which reaches the maintainers privately), or contact a maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled
promptly and confidentially, and the reporter is not retaliated against.

Maintainers will follow these steps, in order of seriousness: a private reminder, a public warning, a temporary
restriction, and a permanent ban from the project's spaces.
